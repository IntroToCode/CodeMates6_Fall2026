# Nutella French Toast

## An American take on a classic French breakfast. It's the perfect hangover cure!

Ingredients:
- [ ] 4 slices white sandwich bread
- [ ] 2 tbsp Nutella
- [ ] 2 large eggs
- [ ] 1/4 cup milk
- [ ] 1/4 tsp vanilla extract
- [ ] 1/4 tsp cinnamon
- [ ] pinch of salt
- [ ] 1 tsp butter
      
<!-- List every ingredient -->

Steps for preparation:

1. Make the sandwiches. Spread 1 tbsp Nutella on each of 2 slices of bread. Leave a small border so it doesn't leak. Top each with another slice and press down gently.
2. Mix the egg batter. In a shallow bowl, whisk the eggs, milk, vanilla, cinnamon and salt until smooth.
3. Heat the pan. Melt the butter in a nonstick skillet over medium-low heat.
4. Dip. Dip each sandwich in the egg mixture for about 5 seconds per side. You want it coated but not soggy.
5. Cook. Cook for 2–3 minutes per side, until golden brown and the Nutella inside is melty. Keep the heat low so the outside doesn't burn before the middle warms up.
6. Serve. Cut each sandwich in half diagonally and serve warm.
